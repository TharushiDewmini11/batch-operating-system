What is a Batch Operating System?

  • A Batch Operating System executes a group of jobs together.
  • Jobs are collected and processed without direct user interaction.
  • Similar jobs are grouped into a batch.
  • Jobs are executed one after another according to a planned order.
  • Users normally do not interact with the system while a job is running.
Key idea: Submit jobs → Wait → System processes them automatically → Receive results

Disadvantages

!

No immediate response
Results may take time.

!

Long waiting time
Users may need to wait for the batch to finish.

!

Debugging difficulties
Errors may only be noticed after processing.

!

Limited interaction
Users cannot easily interact with running jobs.

!

Not suitable for real-time applications
Immediate responses are not its main purpose.
